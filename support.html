How to play

Build runs (one color, numbers in order) and groups (one number, different colors). Your first play must total 30 points from your own tiles. After that, rework anything on the table. First to clear their rack wins. The tutorial is always available in Settings.

Online games

If you lose connection, open Lumiku again and tap Continue game on Home — your match is kept on the server.
