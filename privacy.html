Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Lumiku is a number-tile strategy game. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your display name, avatar, settings, statistics, achievements, match history, Daily Puzzle streak and any saved practice game are stored only on your device.

Online play

When you choose to play online (Quick Play or a private game), the app creates an anonymous account: a random identifier, with no email address, phone number or password. To run matches, our server stores that identifier, your display name and avatar, the rooms you join, and the moves of your online games. This information is used only to operate online play and to keep games fair. It is hosted by Supabase.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or tracking. Lumiku contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Online game records are kept only as long as needed to operate the service. To have your online data deleted, email us with the display name you use in the game.

Children

Lumiku is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
